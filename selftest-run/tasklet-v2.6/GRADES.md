3. Performance improvement observed: not measured (no timings were kept, and there was no comparison).
4. Improvement reproduced: not measured.

## Defects in the method that this run exposed (separate from the grades)

| ID | Defect | Evidence |

| M1 | **The spec gate does not converge, and nothing in the kit stops it.** "Each OPEN ... and the gate runs again" has no end condition and no sense of proportion. Each fresh reader probes a new corner: double BOMs, EPIPE, fsync, file modes, absolute-form request targets. The OPEN count went 10, 4, 1, 3, 3, and even a narrowed verification run found 3 new items. As written, the kit cannot reach an approvable contract for a ~300-line personal tool without the owner departing from it. | `evidence/gate-1.md` to `gate-5.md`; `kit/SKILL.md` Phase 1 step 5; `kit/templates/SPEC-GATE.md` line 9; AMENDMENTS AM-02 and AM-03 (both recorded deviations) |

"Bounded adaptation" says one attempt plus two repairs "per failed gate", and "a check never consumes a repair". Neither says whether the spec gate is a gate under the cap, or whether a contract revision counts as a repair. The Lead read the loop as overriding the cap, which is the direct cause of the check 9 FAIL. | `kit/SKILL.md` lines 129 and 203-205; LOG 12; `BANKED-PROPOSALS.md` T-02 |
